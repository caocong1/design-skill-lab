## What happened

references/process/directions.md section 6 asks for one content.md before the subagents start and
forbids them to invent content. The shared content named 15 of 612 devices and 9 of 40 customers,
while each 1440 x 900 frame needed about 25 table rows. Three directions folded the gap into
"其余 N 台" rows and left empty space; one filled rows by naming pattern. The frames then differed in
density for reasons unrelated to their direction.

## Expected / suggestion

Section 6 could say: content.md holds enough rows to fill the largest frame's main list (and every
state the list distinguishes), or states explicitly that folding rows is the expected treatment.
