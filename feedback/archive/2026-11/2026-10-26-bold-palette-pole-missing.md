## What happened

The original request asked for a redesign with 酷炫 (flashy, impressive) figure motion. Round 1 put five directions on the board. On the colour-strategy axis they were restrained, restrained with dark panels, drenched in one blue, restrained, and brand-green. None was a full palette, and none used 3D imagery or shapes that carry meaning. The user liked the motion but asked for an added exaggerated version: rich colour, richer shapes, and 3D written in WebGPU. That cost a second round (a bolder re-roll per references/process/directions.md §10).

## Expected / suggestion

In references/process/directions.md §5, when the brief's words point at spectacle (酷炫 / 炫酷 / 震撼 / "wow"), check the coordinates table before drawing. At least one direction should reach the far pole of Colour strategy (full palette or drenched with several roles) and of Imagery (illustration / 3D), not only of Motion personality. A spectacle request is about the whole surface, not only the animation.
