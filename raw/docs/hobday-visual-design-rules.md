# Visual design rules you can safely follow every time

> Source: https://anthonyhobday.com/sideprojects/saferules/ ｜ Author: Anthony Hobday ｜ Published: undated ｜ Fetched: 2026-09-21 ｜ Method: curl + text extraction
> This file is a **paraphrased structured digest**, not a verbatim copy. The original illustrates each rule.

## Key rules / claims

1. Near-black and near-white instead of pure black and white.
2. Saturate neutrals slightly with the interface's colour - warm or cool, never both.
3. High contrast for important elements.
4. Everything is deliberate: spacing, alignment, size, colour, shadow.
5. Optical alignment often beats mathematical alignment.
6. Larger text gets lower letter-spacing and line-height; smaller text gets more.
7. A container border must contrast with both the container and the background behind it.
8. Everything aligns with something else.
9. Colours in a palette have distinct brightness values.
10. Measurements are mathematically related (a scale).
11. Order elements by visual weight (heaviest at the outside of a row).
12. A horizontal grid uses 12 columns.
13. Measure spacing between points of high contrast, not between bounding boxes.
14. Closer elements are lighter, in both light and dark themes.
15. Shadow blur is about twice its offset (4 px down -> 8 px blur).
16. Simple on complex, or complex on simple - never complex on complex.
17. Container-to-background brightness difference stays within about 12% in dark interfaces and 7% in light ones.
18. Outer padding is equal to or greater than inner padding.
19. Body text is 16 px or larger.
20. Line length is around 70 characters (60-80 is fine).
21. Button horizontal padding is about twice the vertical padding.
22. Two typefaces at most.
23. Nested corners: inner radius = outer radius minus the gap.
24. Never place two hard divides next to each other.
25. No shadows in dark interfaces.
26. One depth technique per interface.
27. Lower the contrast of an icon paired with text, because icons look heavier.

## What is perishable vs durable

Durable throughout: these are perception rules. The author frames them as safe defaults that may be broken for a reason.

## Notes for the skill

A ready-made lint list for the craft layer of `critique-design` and for `references/layout-and-spacing.md`. Rules 13, 15, 17, 21 and 23 are numeric and directly checkable.
