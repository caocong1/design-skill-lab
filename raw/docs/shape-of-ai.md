## Key rules / claims

The library groups AI interaction patterns into families:

- **Wayfinders** - help users start: example gallery, follow-up questions, initial open-ended CTA, nudges, prompt details (show what happens behind the scenes), randomise, suggestions, templates.
- **Prompt actions** - what users can ask for: auto-fill, chained action, describe, expand, inline action (act on something already on the page), inpainting (regenerate a region), madlibs (structured repeatable prompts), open input, regenerate, restructure, restyle, summary, synthesis, transform (change modality).
- **Tuners** - adjust context and constraints to refine the prompt: attachments, connectors (external data and context), filters (constrain inputs or outputs by source, type, modality), model management, modes, parameters, preset styles, prompt enhancer, saved styles, voice and tone.
- **Governors** - human-in-the-loop oversight and agency: action plan (show the steps before executing), branches, citations, controls (pause or stop mid-stream and adjust), cost estimates, draft mode, memory (control what the AI knows about you), references (see and manage sources), sample response (confirm intent first), shared vision (live visibility of the AI acting in a shared workspace), stream of thought (reasoning, tool use and decisions for auditability), variations, verification (confirm decisions and actions before they proceed).
- **Trust builders** - confidence that results are ethical, accurate and trustworthy: caveat, consent, data ownership, disclosure (mark AI-guided content and interactions), footprints (trace the steps from prompt to result), incognito mode, watermark.
- **Identifiers** - what makes the AI recognisable at brand or model level: avatar, colour, iconography, name, personality.

## What is perishable vs durable

Durable: the questions behind the families - how do I start, what can I ask, how do I steer, how do I stay in control, why should I believe it, what is AI here. Perishable: the concrete patterns and which products exemplify them; this field changes quarter by quarter.
