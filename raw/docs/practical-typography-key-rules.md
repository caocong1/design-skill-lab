# Practical Typography: summary of key rules

> Source: https://practicaltypography.com/summary-of-key-rules.html ｜ Author: Matthew Butterick ｜ Published: web book, continuously revised ｜ Fetched: 2026-09-21 ｜ Method: WebFetch
> This file is a **paraphrased structured digest**, not a verbatim copy.

## Key rules / claims

1. Body text is decided by four things: point size, line spacing, line length and the font.
2. Point size: 10-12 pt in print, 15-25 px on the web.
3. Line spacing: 120-145% of the point size.
4. Line length: 45-90 characters including spaces.
5. A professional font is the most visible single improvement; avoid novelty, monospaced (for body), most free fonts and system defaults such as Times New Roman and Arial.
6. Curly quotation marks and apostrophes; straight marks only for feet and inches.
7. Bold or italic sparingly, never both together; never underline except possibly links.
8. All caps only for less than a line, with 5-12% extra letterspacing (the same for small caps); use real small caps or none.
9. Centre text sparingly.
10. One space between sentences; no runs of whitespace.
11. Kerning always on.
12. Either first-line indents (1-4 times the point size) or space between paragraphs (4-10 pt), never both.
13. Hyphenate justified text.
14. Use real dashes, the ellipsis character, proper trademark and copyright symbols, and non-breaking spaces after paragraph and section marks.
15. Ampersands mostly in proper names; at most one exclamation mark in a long document.

## What is perishable vs durable

All durable. The advice against free fonts predates today's high-quality open-licence families; read it as "avoid the defaults everyone has seen".

## Notes for the skill

The three body-text numbers (size, 120-145% leading, 45-90 characters) are the baseline in `references/typography.md`. CJK text needs different values.
