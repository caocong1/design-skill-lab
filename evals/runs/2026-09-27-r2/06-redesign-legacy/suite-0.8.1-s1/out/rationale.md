# Schedule day view: rationale

**Structure follows the work.** Check-in is 42% of clicks, so the old left column (calendar, legends, waitlist) is now a front-desk queue. It holds patient search, the waiting room with minutes waited (Dr. Rao's question), who is in which room, and the next four patients, each with a **Check in** button.

**Status first, type second.** Cells are tinted and labelled with what the desk needs: *Here 12m*, *Rm 7*, *Unconf.*, ✓, *Cancelled*. The learned type colours are now a left stripe next to a text code (FU, SK…), explained in the key under the grid. Names are bold and larger.

**The problems staff named are visible.** Feld's 10:15 double booking is a red cell with both names and **2×**. Free slots read "+ Open · book". All four clinicians and 8:00–1:30 fit on one screen: no sideways scrolling, and lunch is collapsed. Liu and Soto carry an unverified-insurance flag.

**Alerts on top.** Harold Brennan's panel puts allergy, balance and needs right under his name. Check-in is a checklist: copay $25 is not collected and no room is assigned, so **Collect copay** is the one primary button. No show and Cancel are red.

**Where things went:** mini-calendar → click the date. Print Day, Print Labels, Export → *Print / Export* menu. Preferences, Change Password, Logout, server/build → the *mlopez* user menu. Status legend → words on each cell. Waitlist → *Waitlist 4* tab and *Fill gaps*. Alert bar → *To do today* plus the maintenance line in the header. Footer counts → under the date and in the queue.

**Kept on purpose:** the navy header, the tab names and order, the clinician column order, the toolbar verbs (New Appt, Find Slot, Walk-In, Block Time), Day/Week/Month, and the type colours.
