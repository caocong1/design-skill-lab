# Schedule day view: rationale

**What changed in the structure**

- **Colour shows status, not type.** Green = waiting (with a "12m" timer), blue = in a room ("Rm 7"), dashed amber with "?" = unconfirmed, struck through = cancelled. Type is a thin stripe plus a text code (F/U, Sick…).
- **A front-desk column on the left replaces the calendar and legends.** It holds a large patient search, the waiting room with minutes waited (Dr. Rao's ask), who is in which room, the next arrivals with a one-click **Check in** (42% of clicks), and "Needs attention".
- **Problems sit next to the work.** The Feld 10:15 double booking is a red-bordered cell with a "2" badge and both patients. Unverified insurance is an "INS" tag on Liu and Soto.
- **Patient alerts come first.** Allergy, balance and needs sit right under the name. The 12 same-looking buttons became a check-in checklist ending in **Collect $25.00**.
- **All four clinicians fit with no sideways scroll.** Lunch is one row, so 8:00–12:00 is visible without scrolling. The afternoon scrolls inside the grid.

**Where things went**

- Mini-calendar: the date button.
- Status codes legend: a status and type legend under the grid. Day counts are in the status bar.
- Waitlist: the "Waitlist (4)" tab.
- Alert banner: "Needs attention".
- Print Day, Print Labels, Export: the "Print / Export" menu.
- Preferences, Change Password, Logout: the user menu.
- Maintenance notice, server and build: the status bar.
- Room, Eligibility and Copay: checklist steps. Check In shows as done for an arrived patient.

**What I kept**

The tab labels and order, the provider columns and their order, "Last, First" names, 15-minute rows, the type hues, the navy header, and the button names. Temps still find things where they learned them.
