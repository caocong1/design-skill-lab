# Schedule day view: rationale

**What changed and why**

- **Search first.** Check-in is 42% of clicks, so patient search is now a large box in the top bar.
- **Colour means status, not type.** Green = waiting, blue = in a room, dashed amber = unconfirmed, grey strike-through = cancelled. Each status also has a word ("Waiting 12 min", "Room 7", "? Unconfirmed"). Type stays in the reason text.
- **Left column is the front-desk queue.** It shows:
  - the waiting room, with minutes waited
  - who is in which room
  - who is expected next, each with a Check In button
  - what needs attention: Feld's 10:15 double booking, 2 unverified insurances, unconfirmed patients
  - the waitlist
- **Double bookings are visible.** Feld 10:15 shows both children in a red-outlined "2×" cell.
- **One screen.** All four clinicians and 8:00–1:30 fit without scrolling. Lunch is one row. Free slots read "Open · book" (booking is 23% of clicks).
- **Alerts first.** Allergy, overdue balance and hearing alerts sit under the patient's name. The next steps (Copay $25, Room) come first. Cancel and No Show are red and last.

**Where things went**

- Mini calendar → date button in the toolbar.
- Type and status legends → removed; cells state status in words.
- Print Day, Print Labels, Export → "Print / Export" menu.
- Preferences, Change Password, Logout → "mlopez · Front Desk" menu.
- Alert bar → "Needs attention".
- Maintenance notice, server, build, day totals → footer.
- Refresh → icon button at the end of the toolbar.

**What stayed the same**

- The overall layout: grid in the centre, details on the right.
- The same 14 tabs in the same order.
- Clinician column order and "Last, First" names.
- All 12 appointment actions, with their old labels.
- Toolbar actions, Day/Week/Month, provider filter.
