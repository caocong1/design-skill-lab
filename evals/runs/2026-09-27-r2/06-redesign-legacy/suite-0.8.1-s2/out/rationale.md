# Schedule day view: rationale

**What changed and why**

- **Left panel is the front-desk queue** (check-in is 42% of clicks). *Here now* shows who is waiting and for how long, and who is in which room (Dr. Rao's ask). *Due next* rows each have Check In. *Needs attention* lists the double booking, the unverified insurance and the unconfirmed patients, with Verify and Remind.
- **The grid shows status first.** Each cell says WAITING, IN RM, UNCONF. or CANCELLED in words, plus a matching fill: never colour alone. Names are larger and bold. The type colour stays as a stripe, with its code (F/U, Sick…) in text. Feld 10:15 reads DOUBLE-BOOKED (2) and names both children. Open slots say "Open + Book" (booking is 23%). A red line marks 8:04.
- **All four clinicians fit on one screen**, with no sideways scrolling. The grid scrolls to 1:30.
- **Patient alerts are at the top of the right panel.** Below them, check-in is a list of steps: arrived ✓, insurance ✓, then **Collect Copay** as the one primary button, then Room.
- **Patient search** is the widest toolbar field.

**Where things went**

- Toolbar **More**: Block Time, Print Day, Print Labels, Export.
- Panel **More**: Check Out, Notes, Eligibility, Reminder, Print.
- **Key** holds the type and status legends. The date button opens the calendar. Refresh sits beside "8:04".
- The yellow banner's items are in *Needs attention*, and the maintenance notice is in the status bar. Preferences, Change Password and Logout are in the **mlopez** menu.

**What we kept**

The 14 tabs, with the same names and order. The clinician column order and the 15-minute rows. The legacy button words and the type colours. The waitlist stays in the left column. Temps find things where they learned them.
