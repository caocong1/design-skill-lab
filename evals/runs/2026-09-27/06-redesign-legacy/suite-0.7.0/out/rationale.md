# Schedule day view: rationale

**What changed and why**

- **New "front desk now" column on the left.** Check-in is 42% of clicks, and Dr. Rao asked who is waiting and for how long. It lists waiting patients with wait times, who is in which room, and next arrivals with a **Check in** button.
- **Colour means status, not type.** Waiting is green, in a room is blue, unconfirmed has a dashed amber border. Each also has a label and icon; the key is in the footer. Type is a short tag.
- **Double bookings stand out.** Feld's 10:15 slot has a red border, a "Double-booked · 2" badge and both children listed.
- **Faster to find a patient:** larger names and a big search box top left.
- **Patient alerts come first** in the detail panel, followed by a check-in checklist. The next step, **Collect copay $25.00**, is the only filled button there. Cancel and No-show sit apart, in red.
- **"To follow up"** takes over the yellow alert bar and adds the double booking.

**Where functions went**

- **Tab bar "More":** Tasks, Reports, Inventory and Admin.
- **"mlopez" menu:** Preferences, Change password and Logout.
- **Toolbar "Print & more":** Print day, Print labels, Export and Block time.
- **Date button:** opens the calendar.
- **Above the grid:** Refresh and the provider filter.
- **Footer:** maintenance notice and server/build details.
- **New appointment / Find slot:** the appointment-type durations from the legend.

**What we kept**

- The three-part layout: context on the left, the day grid in the middle, details on the right.
- The navy header and the tab order.
- All four clinicians on one screen, in the same order, with their rooms.
- 15-minute rows, "Last, First" names and Day/Week/Month.
- All twelve appointment actions in the right panel.
