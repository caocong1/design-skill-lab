# Schedule day view: rationale

**What changed, and why**

- **Colour shows status, not type.** Green means waiting (with minutes), blue means in a room, dashed amber with "?" means unconfirmed, and a struck-through name means cancelled. Each also has an icon or words. Type keeps its old colour as a thin stripe, so learned meanings hold.
- **Who is here comes first.** The left column shows the waiting room with wait times, who is in a room, who is late, and what needs attention: the Feld 10:15 double booking, the two unverified insurances (Liu, Soto) and the unconfirmed patients. Column headers count each clinician's waiting and roomed patients.
- **Finding people.** A large search box sits top left. Names are bigger (13–14 px, was 10), mixed case, surname bold.
- **Double bookings show.** Feld 10:15 has a taller row and a red box holding both children.
- **Alerts before actions.** The allergy, overdue balance and hearing note sit at the top of the right panel. Below them are the details to confirm, then one large **Finish Check In** button naming the $25 copay. The other eleven buttons are smaller; No Show and Cancel are red.
- **One screen.** All four clinicians and 8:00–1:30 fit without scrolling. Lunch takes one thin row, and free slots read "+ Open".

**Where things went**

- Block Time, Print Day, Print Labels, Export: **More** menu.
- Mini calendar: the date button.
- Type and status legends: **Key** (grid corner).
- Preferences, Change Password, Logout: **mlopez** menu.
- Maintenance notice: Messages. Server/build: Help › About.
- Search "Go": Enter.

**What we kept**

The same 14 tabs, labels and order; the same toolbar verbs and button names, the four columns in the same order, the type colours, and the details panel on the right.
