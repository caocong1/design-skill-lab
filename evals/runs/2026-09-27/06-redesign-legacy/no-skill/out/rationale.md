# Schedule day view: rationale

**What changed**

- **Left column shows who is here:** Waiting room with minutes waited, In a room, and Arriving next with a **Check in** button per row. Check-in is 42% of clicks, and Dr. Rao asked for the waiting room.
- **Patient search is the first and largest toolbar control**; arrivals are found fast.
- **Grid colour means status, not type.** Waiting is green and In room blue, with minutes. Unconfirmed is amber and dashed; cancelled is struck through. Every status is also in words. Type is a short text label. Names are larger.
- **Double bookings are marked:** Feld 10:15 shows both children and a "2 booked" tag.
- **Unverified insurance is tagged** "Verify ins." on Liu and Soto.
- **All four clinicians fit without scrolling.** Lunch collapses to one band, so 8:00 AM–1:45 PM fits, with a red "Now 8:04" line. Open slots read "+ Open" for booking (23%).
- **Patient alerts sit directly under the name** at the top.
- **The twelve identical buttons became a checklist.** For Harold: insurance verified, copay not collected (green **Collect copay**), room not assigned. The other actions sit below; No show and Cancel are red.

**Where things went**

- Date ▾: mini calendar.
- More ▾: Block time, Print day, Print labels, Export, Refresh.
- mlopez ▾: Preferences, Change password, Logout.
- Help › About: server and build.
- Left column: the yellow alerts and the waitlist (under Needs follow-up), day totals and the maintenance notice.
- The type and status legends are gone, because cells now use words.

**Kept on purpose**

- All fourteen tabs in the same order ("Insurance / Eligibility" shortened).
- The same clinician columns and 15-minute rows.
- "Last, First" names.
- The details panel on the right, with every field.
