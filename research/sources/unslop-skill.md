## Key facts
1. **Scope.** A skill for Claude Code (plugin), Codex and Google Antigravity that turns "vibecoded, AI-looking UI" into an interface "with its own character", on React, Tailwind, HTML and CSS, for dashboards, apps and landing pages. It keeps the product: "The features, content and order of sections stay."
2. **Thesis.** Removing slop is half the job, because a clean screen "that could belong to any product is the next template"; slop "spreads color and effects over the chrome", craft "keeps the chrome quiet and puts the richness into one or two moments of content" (a rendered object, a chart, an image from the product's world).
3. **Run.** (1) Look at the rendered screen (a URL can be saved with `scripts/snapshot.mjs`, which also screenshots the live page; a screenshot alone is rebuilt as static HTML first). (2) Audit against `references/signs.md`, one line per sign with where it lives (RTL screens read `references/rtl.md` first). (3) Decide how much to change: heavy slop runs everything; light slop is "polish mode" (fix only what the audit found, keep palette, type, layout and visual; bigger ideas go into the report); no slop means saying so and offering at most two or three refinements, "Never change a good screen just to make it different." (4) Write the direction and **stop for the user's answer** unless told to just do it. (5) Nine passes in order, each across the whole screen. (6) Re-render and compare with the audit list. (7) Report. At most three renders per run.
4. **Direction in five lines**, derived "from what the page says the product does", "Never from what is trending": Product (one sentence); Character (two or three concrete words; "clean, modern, sleek, minimal and premium are not allowed"); World (the place or material the product belongs to, which sets the neutral tint, the accent and the visual); Moments (one or two places that get rich colour and detail); Signature (one small detail only this product would have).
5. **Pass order and why.** Spacing, colour, icons, copy, type, layout, details, visuals, motion: spacing first because everything reads differently once the rhythm is right; copy before type and layout because the headline's length decides its size; visuals and motion last.
6. **Values it sets** (its house rules, not universal): a 4 px grid; navigation rows 32–36 tall; padding about 16 in compact panels, 20–24 in cards, 24–32 at page edges; one neutral ramp tinted toward the world, neutral surfaces in apps, three text roles, one rare accent at 4.5:1; borders as hairlines at 6–10 % of the text colour; icons 16 or 18 px at a 1.5 stroke in the text colour; weights 400 and 500, 600 at most; sentence case; no negative tracking on small text; tabular figures; nested radius = outer − padding, never under 4; controls 8–10, cards 12–16; one easing curve, at most one entrance on load.
7. **Checks.** A checklist ending with the **template check**: "If you swapped the product name for another product's, would the screen still fit? If yes, push the direction harder in the copy, the visual and the signature detail." Do-not list: do not rename the product, do not change what it is, "do not replace removed decoration with new decoration", "do not swap one trend for another like glass, grain, noise, neon or bento grids", do not invent features, claims or data.
8. **Signs list** (`references/signs.md`) pairs each sign with its fix, grouped by surfaces and colour, type, icons, spacing and more: purple-to-blue or violet-to-pink gradients, gradient text, coloured glows, radial washes and blobs, neon borders, many near-identical tints, everything highlighted, glass cards with a white inner highlight; 800–900 heading weights, tracked uppercase labels, mixed number formats, negative tracking on small text, italic emphasis; emoji as icons or in counts, a sparkle for anything AI; arbitrary spacing values and uneven rows.
9. **Story video.** Optional: a square, about 10-second recording of the screen "unslopping itself" (CSS steps that tween in place, red redlines and measurements), rendered with Playwright and ffmpeg (`scripts/story.mjs`); every video carries an `unslop by @heydamir` credit. Optional hero imagery through an image tool, with the prompt shown in the report.
